## Overview

Traditional organized philosophies and spiritual structures routinely succumb to structural decay: personality cults, doctrinal schisms, rent-seeking institutions, and geographic or linguistic insularity.

**The Open Protocol** is a web-native, open-source philosophical framework designed without intermediaries, teachers, hierarchies, or financial mechanisms. Its principles are taught through direct empirical inquiry, accessible prose, and **interactive canvas simulations** illustrating the mechanics of consciousness, ego-contraction, and emotional friction.

---

## Core Pillars & Features

- **Empirical Phenomenology:** Claims are presented as testable behavioral experiments rather than untestable dogma.
- **Kinetic State Diagrams:** Three interactive HTML5 canvas engines:
  1. *The Cycle of Reaction vs. Response* (Wave dispersal vs. projectile collision).
  2. *Ego Contraction & The Field* (N-body gravitational harmony vs. high-tension isolation).
  3. *The Thermodynamics of Attachment* (Velocity friction vs. dynamic non-attachment).
- **The Seven Open Axioms:** A structured philosophical codex paired with actionable physical practices.
- **Comparative Epistemic Matrix:** A multi-dimensional structural comparison evaluating Traditional Theism, Dogmatic Materialism, and The Open Protocol.
- **In-Browser Card Generator:** Generates exportable, high-resolution PNG summary cards for offline reflection and distributed sharing.
- **Self-Replicating Mirror Engine:** Built-in one-click tools to export the entire runtime as a standalone offline `.html` file or `.md` codex.

---

## Architecture

The entire client is constructed as a **zero-dependency, single-file static application**:
- **Markup:** Semantic HTML5 with accessibility landmark roles.
- **Styling:** CSS variables, responsive fluid grid layouts, and glassmorphic surface treatments.
- **Logic:** Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript driving pure mathematical calculations and standard HTML5 Canvas 2D contexts.
- **Backend:** None. Zero analytics trackers, zero external fonts or CDN script tags, zero telemetry, and zero cookie storage.
