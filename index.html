The Seven Open Axioms

Grounded mechanical principles paired with actionable daily somatic physical practices.

Consciousness is an open clearing through which phenomena flow, not a proprietor who possesses them. Thoughts, bodily sensations, worldly accomplishments, and material forms are transient travelers. Suffering is the thermodynamic friction of awareness attempting to freeze or claim title over that which is in perpetual transition.

Somatic Practice: When experiencing sudden mental or physical friction, open both palms flat. Whisper mentally: "This sensation has entered the room; it does not belong to me; it will leave on its own accord."

Between any external impulse and the resulting biological reaction lies an infinitesimal temporal gap. The unconscious mind behaves as a deterministic reflex automaton. Human sovereignty and moral freedom exist strictly in the conscious widening of this micro-interval.

Somatic Practice: When triggered, insulted, or provoked, enforce two full tidal respiratory cycles before voluntary vocal or muscular engagement.

No human teacher, priest, prophet, guru, or algorithmic intermediary possesses privileged or proprietary bandwidth to reality. Any institution that charges fees, demands personal submission, or claims exclusive keys to truth is an extractive bottleneck.

Somatic Practice: Consult direct first-person experimentation over revered authorities. Never tithe to spiritual organizations; provide food, tools, or physical shelter directly to those in need.

Compassion and active goodwill are not sentimental favors granted to others; they are baseline neurobiological hygiene. Malice, resentment, and envy flood the cardiovascular system with inflammatory tension; active benevolence immediately downregulates autonomic strain.

Somatic Practice: Silently extend unconditional ease toward three people who irritated you today. Note the immediate drop in jaw clenching and diaphragmatic compression.

The ego is a continuous metabolic process, not a static stone monument. Treating opinions, political affiliations, and identity labels as immutable structures causes fragility and panic when external conditions shift.

Somatic Practice: Identify one cherished intellectual conviction each week and articulate two respectful counter-arguments with genuine intellectual honesty.

A diagram points to water; it cannot quench thirst. This web protocol succeeds only when it prompts the participant to close the screen and engage tangible reality. Digital networks simulate community while maximizing nervous exhaustion.

Somatic Practice: Close the browser after reading. Step onto bare ground or concrete. Touch plant matter, greet an elder, and drink cold water with full somatic awareness.

A philosophy that cannot be freely copied or mirrored without permission is a brand, not a universal truth. The Commons carries no copyright, no trademark, and no canonical central server. Universal replication renders the movement uncapturable and immortal.

Somatic Practice: Save this application locally. Host it on your independent domain, distribute it on USB keys, or print physical folios for your local community.
