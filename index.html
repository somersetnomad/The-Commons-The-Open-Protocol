Peer Assembly Protocol

How to host autonomous, leaderless study circles locally or online without guru dynamics, financial capture, or dogma.

I. Core Ethos & The Anti-Guru Mandate

An assembly of this protocol is never a lecture, sermon, or therapy workshop. It is a peer laboratory where individuals review their personal experiments in conduct.

  • No Head of Table: Sit in an unbroken circle (physical or digital). The person initiating the meeting is purely a chronological timekeeper, not an enlightened master.
  • Strict Timekeeper Rotation: The duty of ringing the bell and moderating time must rotate every single session.
  • Zero Extraction: Gatherings are unconditionally free. No donations, collection bowls, or sales of books, coaching, or courses.
  • The Talking Threshold: Only one person speaks at a time without interruption, cross-talk, or unsolicited advice. Speakers describe personal experiments, not theoretical abstractions.

II. Standard 60-Minute Session Architecture

00:00 - 00:05 | THE OBSERVER INTERVAL Begin with 5 minutes of total silence. Synchronized physical breathing (4s in, 6s out) to decompress from screen/city velocity. 00:05 - 00:15 | FOLIO SELECTION & DIAGRAM STUDY Read one of the 36 Folios aloud twice. Open the modal teaching on screen and review the mechanical cause and effect in the animation. 00:15 - 00:50 | SOVEREIGN DIALOGUE ROUND Each participant takes up to 4 minutes to answer: 1. "Where did I observe this friction in my own conduct this week?" 2. "What was the measurable output when I applied the somatic praxis?" (No commentary, debating, or unsolicited counseling from other participants). 00:50 - 00:60 | SOMATIC SILENT CLOSE Review the tangible commitment for the upcoming week. Complete silence for the final 3 minutes. Disperse cleanly into physical reality.

III. Digital Circles via Whereby

To maintain complete friction-free accessibility without forcing attendees to register corporate accounts or install proprietary heavy software, host digital assemblies using Whereby:

  • Create a room URL (e.g. whereby.com/commons-study-[city]). Attendees join with a single click in any browser.
  • Set Room Mode to Small Group (grid view where all webcam boxes are equal size).
  • Share your screen during the first 15 minutes to run the real-time kinetic diagram together.
