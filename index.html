Codex of Direct Seeing

The 7 Foundational Axioms

These are not divine commandments. They are working hypotheses formulated for the sovereign human mind. Test them; if they fail your inspection, discard them without remorse.

Direct sensory awareness and direct cognitive honesty precede all scripture. Any book, priest, or algorithm that places itself between your bare awareness and what is actually occurring right now is an extortion of presence.

Practice: When fear arises, do not repeat a dogma. Strip the conceptual label away and feel the raw physical vibration in your chest without judgment.

Morality is not enforced by an angry sky magistrate with a ledger. Action carries its own immediate metabolic and environmental echo. Greed poisons the nervous system of the hoarder before it deprives the community. Cruelty corrodes the vessel that holds it.

Practice: Notice the physiological contraction that instantly follows a calculated lie. The punishment is not delayed; it is immediate contraction of being.

Truth belongs to nobody. It cannot be patented, copyrighted, trademarked, or cordoned off behind esoteric paywalls. Anyone who charges admission for your own fundamental nature is a merchant in a temple that should be cleared.

Practice: Freely replicate, critique, translate, and distribute wisdom without fear of institutional reprisal.

Do not send money to a centralized fund. Do not build monuments. If your practice does not translate into feeding the hungry person within walking distance, comforting your neighbor, or caring for the soil beneath your boots, it is mere theatrical vanity.

Practice: Direct, anonymous assistance given locally with zero receipt and zero public display.

Reactivity is the mechanical trait of an automaton. Human dignity begins in the tiny, pregnant pause between receiving an offense and replying. Cultivate that space like sacred ground. It is the only seat where free will truly exists.

Practice: Count one steady inhalation before responding to any provocative digital or physical word.

Stars explode; bodies age; empires turn to chalk. This is not a tragedy inflicted upon existence; it is the engine of its beauty. To demand permanence from what is inherently transient is the single root of all neurosis.

Practice: Greet every loss not as an injustice, but as the return of a loan to the universal ledger.

A healthy raft is abandoned once the opposite shore is reached. This website, these diagrams, and these concepts are mere scaffolds. If you begin worshipping The Commons or quoting it like holy canon, turn off your device and walk outside.

Practice: Delete your bookmarks once presence becomes second nature. Never declare yourself an 'adherent'.
